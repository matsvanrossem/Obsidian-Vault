### Lesson sequence, 2 VWO

| Time      | EDI phase                | Teacher actions                                                                                                                                       | Student actions                                                        | Stepping Stones / Other                                |
| --------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0-3 min   | **Lesson start**         | Welcome students, establish attention and explain the lesson plan.                                                                                    | Prepare materials and get their reading book ready.                    | Lesson plan visible on the board.                      |
| 3-7 min   | **Word of the Week**     | Introduce the Word of the Week from Classroom. Discuss meaning, pronunciation and an example sentence.                                                | Listen, repeat the word and use it in a short sentence.                | Word of the Week in Classroom.                         |
| 7-27 min  | **Independent reading**  | Start the weekly 20-minute reading session. Monitor reading and support where needed.                                                                 | Read independently for 20 minutes.                                     | 1 x per week: 20 minutes reading.                      |
| 27-32 min | **Reading check-in**     | Ask students to briefly note what they read and one thing they remember from the text.                                                                | share with a partner.                                                  | Independent reading.                                   |
| 32-37 min | **Test planning**        | Explain that the Chapter 1 test needs to be scheduled in Magister for the week after the autumn holiday. Make the test date visible if already known. | Note the test period/date in their planner.                            | Chapter 1 test: week after the autumn holiday.         |
| 37-42 min | **Study preparation**    | Explain that irregular verbs 1-29 need to be learned for the test. Briefly review a few examples and how students can study them.                     | Check which irregular verbs they already know and mark difficult ones. | Irregular verbs 1-29.                                  |
| 42-44 min | **Lesson closure**       | Ask students what they need to prepare for the Chapter 1 test.                                                                                        | State the homework and test preparation in their own words.            | Test preparation.                                      |
| 44-45 min | **Exit ticket/homework** | Ask students to write down the homework and one irregular verb they still need to practise.                                                           | Complete the exit ticket and note homework.                            | **Homework: Learn irregular verbs 1-29 for the test.** |

### Notes
- **1 x per week:** 20 minutes of independent reading.
- **Word of the Week:** Classroom.
- **Chapter 1 test:** schedule in Magister for the week after the autumn holiday.
