### Strategies for Reading
- **Predict the topic:** Use the title, pictures and headings to predict the subject.
- **Skim first:** Read quickly for the main idea without stopping at every unfamiliar word.
- **Scan for details:** Look for key words, names, numbers or places when a question asks for specific information.
- **Use context:** Read the words around an unfamiliar word to work out its likely meaning.
