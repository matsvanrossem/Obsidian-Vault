## Differentiation

**Students who need additional support**

- Use a small word bank drawn from the official illness and injuries vocabulary list.
- Sort target words into meaningful categories before writing sentences.
- Receive model sentences and immediate correction of common word-choice errors.
- Finish the essential exercise items with teacher support before attempting extension work.

**Students ready for extension**

- Compare easily confused terms and explain which context fits each term.
- Write a short health-situation description using at least four target words.
- Make a mini-quiz that combines *At the doctor's* with *Illness and injuries* vocabulary.

## Assessment and follow-up

- **Evidence of learning:** Exercise responses, vocabulary checkpoint, and two-part exit ticket.
- **Possible misconception to watch:** Students use a general health word where a more specific illness or injury term is required.
- **Homework / preparation:** Study both **Theme Words B** sets: *At the doctor's* and *Illness and injuries*. Preview **C Reading** and its vocabulary for Lesson 3.
