## Differentiation

**Students who need additional support**

- Read the assigned text in shorter sections, with pauses to state the gist of each section.
- Use a simple frame: 'This text is mainly about ... and says that ...'.
- Highlight repeated ideas and key sentences before deciding on the main idea.
- Receive explicit feedback when an answer names only a topic or an isolated detail.

**Students ready for extension**

- Write a main-idea statement without copying a sentence from the text.
- Support the statement with two details from different parts of the text.
- Explain why a plausible but too-specific alternative does not capture the whole text.

## Assessment and follow-up

- **Evidence of learning:** Reasoning during exercises 17-21, one-sentence main idea at the checkpoint, and exit ticket on the strategy.
- **Possible misconception to watch:** Confusing the topic or an interesting detail with the main idea of the whole text.
- **Homework / preparation:** Study **vocabulary C**, review the **main idea** reading strategy, and continue revising **Theme Words B** from this week. The following week begins D Speaking & Stones.
