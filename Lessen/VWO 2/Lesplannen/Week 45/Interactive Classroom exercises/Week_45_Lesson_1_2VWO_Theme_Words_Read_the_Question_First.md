# Lesson blueprint: 2 VWO | Week 45, Lesson 1 

**Subject:** English  
**Class:** 2 VWO  
**Coursebook:** Stepping Stones  
**Theme:** Theme 2: Health  
**Section:** B Theme Words, *At the doctor's*  
**Scheduled exercises:** 10-13  
**Lesson duration:** 45 minutes  
**Teacher:** Mats van Rossem __________  
**Date:** 4 Nov 2026 

## Preparation

| Element | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| **Main learning objective** | Students can identify what a vocabulary question requires **before** answering it, and then use Theme Words B accurately. |
| **Success criteria** | **I can:** (1) circle the **command word**; (2) mark the **answer direction/format** (English or Dutch; word, choice, or sentence); (3) notice **special conditions** (number, *not*, tense); (4) check that my answer fits the instruction; (5) correctly apply at least four *At the doctor's* words. |
| **Key vocabulary** | *appointment, cast, crutches, dentist, GP, plaster, prescription, surgeon, to examine, waiting room, X-ray*; select 6-8 for direct practice. These words come from the supplied Theme Words B list. |
| **Materials** | Theme Words B vocabulary sheet, Stepping Stones exercises 10-13, the accompanying **Question First** student task cards, board/projector, mini-whiteboards or paper, highlighters or pencils. |
| **Prior knowledge** | Week 44 introduction to Health. Students have seen vocabulary and grammar test questions in Chapter 1. |
| **Assessment focus** | Record **task comprehension separately from vocabulary accuracy** to find out why students get answers wrong. |

## Lesson sequence (45 minutes, EDI + active working methods)

| Time          | Phase / method                                     | Teacher actions                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Student actions and evidence                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **0-4 min**   | **Hook: Right word, wrong task**                   | Show: **“Translate wachtkamer into English.”** Student A answers *wachtkamer*; Student B answers *waiting room*. Ask: “Why would one lose a mark even if they recognise the word?” Do not frame it as a general reading text.                                                                                 | Think, vote A/B, explain the **direction** mistake to a partner.                                                                                                                          |
| **4-7 min**   | **Activate prior knowledge: Spot the instruction** | Show three short instructions: *Choose the correct word*; *Write TWO words*; *Write the word that does NOT fit*. Ask students to say what a successful answer would look like **without answering**.                                                                                                          | Mini-whiteboard response: *choose / write two / select the exception*. Teacher notes how many immediately miss *TWO* or *NOT*.                                                            |
| **7-13 min**  | **Explicit teaching and modelling: TASK**          | Introduce the four TASK steps. Think aloud with *wachtkamer*, then lead a second model using *GP = huisarts*. Rapidly introduce/retrieve 6-8 B vocabulary items so students have words to work with.                                                                                                          | Circle/box/underline on the sheet. Tell a partner the language and answer form **before** providing the vocabulary answer.                                                                |
| **13-20 min** | **Guided practice: Instruction sort**              | Give pairs student cards **1-4**. Ask them to put them in groups: *translate*, *choose*, *complete*, *correct*. Prompt: “Which exact word told you?” Check all four types together.                                                                                                                           | Pair-sort, mark T/A/S, and explain the expected answer format. **No solving until both partners agree on the task.**                                                                      |
| **20-30 min** | **Collaborative practice: Decode, Do, Check**      | Groups of three use student cards **5-10**. Roles: **Decoder** marks the question; **Solver** answers; **Checker** rereads instructions and verifies the response. Rotate roles every two cards. Offer the vocabulary list openly for the first round: this isolates instruction-reading from word knowledge. | Each group marks, explains, answers, and checks. Students say: **“The task is ___; I need to write ___ in ___.”** Teacher listens for confusion about *NOT*, number, or answer direction. |
| **30-38 min** | **Transfer: Real Stepping Stones exercises 10-13** | Direct students to **B Theme Words, exercises 10-13**. Before tackling each instruction, require T/A/S markings and a quick partner explanation. Do not presume the wording of these exercises; use the instructions actually printed in the book/digital platform.                                           | Independently decode the instruction, then complete as many assigned exercise items as time allows. Partners check the **instruction fit first**, then the word.                          |
| **38-42 min** | **Wrong-answer clinic + grammar bridge**           | Display two flawed responses from the teacher key. First: *Write the English word for huisarts*: response *huisarts*. Second: *Complete with the **past simple** of go: Yesterday I ___ to the GP*: response *was going*. Ask: “Is the issue reading the task, knowing the word/rule, or both?”               | Think-pair-share. Correct the responses: *GP* and *went*. Explain that the **same TASK routine** works in vocabulary **and grammar** questions. No grammar reteaching.                    |
| **42-45 min** | **Exit ticket: Decode before answering**           | Give the exit ticket from the student sheet. Collect **both** the markings and the answers. Categorise errors: **I = instruction**, **V = vocabulary**, **B = both**, and (if applicable) **G = grammar form**.                                                                                               | Independently mark one prompt and answer it. Name one TASK step they will use in the next test.                                                                                           |
