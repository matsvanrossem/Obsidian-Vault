## My strategy: TASK

**Before I answer a question:**

- **T | Task word:** Circle what I must do: *translate, choose, complete, correct, write*.
- **A | Answer form:** Box the language or form: *English, Dutch, one word, a choice, a sentence*.
- **S | Special conditions:** Underline anything extra: *TWO, NOT, past simple, only*.
- **K | Keep checking:** Reread the question. Does my answer do **exactly** what it asks?
