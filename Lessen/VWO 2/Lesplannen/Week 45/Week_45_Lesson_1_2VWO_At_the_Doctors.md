## Differentiation

**Students who need additional support**

- Work with a smaller selection of high-frequency words taken from the assigned B vocabulary list.
- Use visual cues, word banks and sentence starters.
- Receive a short guided recap during independent practice.
- Check meaning before being asked to produce complete sentences.

**Students ready for extension**

- Explain the difference between similar words where the coursebook provides them.
- Write a short doctor-patient exchange incorporating at least four of the lesson's target words.
- Create two vocabulary questions for a partner and provide an answer key.

## Assessment and follow-up

- **Evidence of learning:** Checks during exercises 10-13, three-part checkpoint, and exit ticket.
- **Possible misconception to watch:** Students recognise a word in a list but cannot choose or use it in context.
- **Homework / preparation:** Study **Theme Words B: At the doctor's** and preview the vocabulary for **illness and injuries**, which is the focus of Lesson 2.
