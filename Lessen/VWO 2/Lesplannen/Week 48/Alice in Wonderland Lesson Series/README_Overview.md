# Alice in Wonderland | Three-part English lesson series (30 minutes each)

**Class:** 2 VWO  
**Length:** **3 × 30 minutes (90 minutes total)**  
**Literary anchor:** Lewis Carroll, *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* (1865), Chapters 1, 5 and 7.  
**Stepping Stones connection:** Theme 2: Health; Stones 4–6; Grammar 3 (present perfect); Grammar 4 (*can, could, should, have to*); G Reading (false friends); Yellow Pages, pp. 196–197.

## Three-lesson overview

| Lesson | Main story and strategy | In-class core | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Down the Rabbit-Hole** | Skimming and scanning; *sensible / eventually* | Workbook A1–A2, B3–B4, C5, D7–D8, one advice sentence | C6, a second advice sentence and health-feelings sentences |
| **2. Advice from a Caterpillar** | Scanning, context clues; *sympathetic / eventually* | A1–A2, B3–B4, C6, quick D role-play | B5, C7, written consultation |
| **3. A Mad Tea-Party** | Intensive reading; linking words; *actual* | A1–A3, B4, C6–C7, **start** final letter | Complete B5 and the **70–90-word letter** with self-check |

## Recurring teaching strands

Each lesson still revisits: (1) reading purpose and evidence, (2) vocabulary from context, (3) false friends, (4) present perfect and modals, (5) Health-themed communication. Reuse the **TASK** routine: **T**ask, **A**nswer format, **S**pecial conditions, **K**eep checking.

## How this differs from the 45-minute version

- Explicit teaching, reading and speaking time have been shortened, not removed.
- Each lesson has a **clearly defined essential task set**; other workbook items are optional or homework.
- In lesson 3 students **start** the letter during the lesson and finish it at home. The original 70–90-word requirements and 8-point rubric are retained rather than compressing the assessment unrealistically into six minutes.
- Use a visible timer, fixed pairs and short transitions.
