# Lesson 2: Advice from a Caterpillar | Teaching from the Novel Lesson Series

**Subject:** English  |  **Class:** 2 VWO  |  **Duration:** 30 minutes  

**Chapter:** V, the opening conversation with the Caterpillar.  
**Text length:** 149 words.  
**Stepping Stones connections:** Stones 4–6 (feelings, events and advice); Grammar 3 present perfect; Grammar 4 modals; context clues and false friends.

## Learning objective and success criteria

**Objective:** Students recognise a character's main problem and tone in the original dialogue and practise describing experiences and providing reassurance.

I can explain Alice's difficulty in one sentence, copy a clue word-for-word, understand *sternly* using context, complete two present perfect forms and give two pieces of supportive advice.

## Materials and preparation

Reader Lesson 2, pair roles (Question Decoder / Evidence Finder), Laptops.

## 30-minute EDI sequence

| Time | Phase | Teacher actions | Student actions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Retrieval | Ask for last lesson's main idea and an example with *should*. | Write two short retrieval answers. |
| 3–6 | Starter | Display **"Who are you?"** and read the historical context note. | Predict the conversation (Q1). |
| 6–12 | I do: skimming and voice | Read first lines with gentle character voices; model interpreting *shyly* vs *sternly*. | Read whole original and answer Q2. |
| 12–18 | We do: TASK/evidence | Model Q3 **copy ONE phrase** then check Q4's answer format and Q5 context. | Decoder/Evidence Finder roles, Q3–Q5. |
| 18–22 | False friends | Check teacher-written examples separately from novel; clarify *actual/eventually*. | Complete Q6 and justify one choice. |
| 22–27 | Grammar and advice | Distinguish original **must have been changed** from *has changed*. Model *has + participle*, *doesn't have to + base*. | Complete Q7–Q8 independently. |
| 27–30 | Closure | Invite one support sentence, collect exit ticket. | Complete Q9; report evidence. |

**Homework:** Review unfamiliar vocabulary and upload at least two words to google classroom you are unfamiliar with or would like to use more; 8-line dialogue between Alice and a friend including one present perfect form and two modals.
