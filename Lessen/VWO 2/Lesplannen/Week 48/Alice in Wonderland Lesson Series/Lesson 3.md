# Lesson 3: A Mad Tea-Party | Teaching from the Novel Lesson Series
**Subject:** English  |  **Class:** 2 VWO  |  **Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Chapter:** VII, a conversation about politeness.  
**Text length:** 127 words.  
**Stepping Stones connections:** G Reading (intensive reading, false friends), linking words, Stones 4–6, Grammar 3 present perfect and Grammar 4 modals.

## Learning objective and success criteria

**Objective:** Students read authentic dialogue closely, recognise a modal in Carroll's text, distinguish meanings of a linking word and begin a supportive writing task using course grammar and vocabulary.

I can describe the main problem, copy one sentence of advice exactly, find two rude actions, explain two uses of *but*, and write an opening with the present perfect and a modal.

## Materials and preparation

Reader Lesson 3; highlighters, two-colour pens, exit slips. Pre-teach *civil* = beleefd, *remark* = opmerking, *invited* = uitgenodigd; present them as a separate mini-glossary. Avoid implying that the novel itself contains all the later grammar examples.

## 30-minute EDI sequence

| Time | Phase | Teacher actions | Student actions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Starter | Question on board: **What is one polite way to disagree?** | Write one rule (Q1). |
| 3–6 | Explore | Name characters and context; predict disagreement. | Read outside-text context note. |
| 6–12 | I do: intensive reading | Read the original dialogue (teacher as Hatter/Hare, volunteer as Alice). Model identifying tone and a claim. | Read and complete Q2. |
| 12–18 | We do: evidence and linking words | Model Q3's **exactly ONE sentence**; check examples for Q4. Clarify *but* can be contrast or *except*. | Complete Q3–Q5 with partner and evidence. |
| 18–22 | Language and false friends | Contrast *civil* with *rude*; revise *actual* in teacher sentence. | Complete Q6. |
| 22–27 | Grammar and writing | Ask pupils to notice *should learn* directly in novel. Revise present perfect and write a model opening separate from original. | Complete Q7–Q8 independently. |
| 27–30 | Exit / homework | Check Q9 and explain 70–90 word final task. | Complete Q9; note homework conditions. |

**Homework:** Review unfamiliar vocabulary and upload at least two words to google classroom you are unfamiliar with or would like to use more;
 Finish the reader's **70–90 word** advice letter use at least 3 words from the list of words you did not know or wanted to use more.
