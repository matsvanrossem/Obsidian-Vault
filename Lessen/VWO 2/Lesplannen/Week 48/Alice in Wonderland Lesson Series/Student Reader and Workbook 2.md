## TASK: Read every instruction first

**T** Task word: underline *choose, copy, explain, complete, write*.  
**A** Answer format: letter, word, sentence, or short paragraph?  
**S** Special conditions: *exactly two*, *from the text*, *in English*, *present perfect*.  
**K** Keep checking: does your answer match the instruction?

Do not translate every unfamiliar word. First explore the text, then skim for its main idea, scan for evidence, and read a short passage intensively when needed.
