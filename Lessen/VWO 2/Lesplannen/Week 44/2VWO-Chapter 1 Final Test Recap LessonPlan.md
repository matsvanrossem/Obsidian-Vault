| 34-36 min | **Check for understanding**     | Give all students the same short checkpoint: 1 grammar item, 1 irregular verb, 1 vocabulary item and 1 holiday-language item. Decide who still needs final support. | Answer simultaneously and compare with the correct answer. | Mixed Chapter 1 check. |

Remind students to study only the topics they still need before the test. | Complete exit ticket and note personal revision target. | Homework: targeted Chapter 1 revision for the test. |

### Differentiation

**Students who need the recap**
- Join the teacher-led recap after the diagnostic check.
- Receive short, explicit explanations of the grammar rules.
- Practise with scaffolded examples.
- Focus first on:
  1. past simple;
  2. past continuous;
  3. choosing between the two;
  4. common irregular verbs;
  5. essential Chapter 1 vocabulary and Stones phrases.
- Receive immediate feedback and extra teacher support.

**Students who do not need the recap**
- Start independent mixed practice after the diagnostic check.
- Complete more challenging tasks rather than repeating basic instruction.
- Possible extension tasks:
  - correct grammar mistakes;
  - create test-style questions for a classmate;
  - write a short holiday story using both past simple and past continuous;
  - include at least five Chapter 1 vocabulary items and several irregular verbs;
  - review vocabulary or irregular verbs they personally find difficult.

### Chapter 1 test checklist

Students should be able to:

- use the **past simple**;
- use the **past continuous**;
- explain and apply the difference between **past simple and past continuous**;
- know the required **irregular verbs** from p. 218-219;
- know the vocabulary for **travelling**;
- know the vocabulary for **packing**;
- know the vocabulary for **holiday activities**;
- know the vocabulary for **weather**;
- know **Words A, C, F and G**;
- use the **Stones phrases about holidays**;
- describe a **past holiday** and explain what happened.
