### Past Simple vs. Past Continuous

**Past simple** is used for:

- a finished action in the past
    
- a short event that happened
    

Examples:

- I **watched** a film yesterday.
    
- She **called** me last night.
    
- The bus **arrived** at 8 o’clock.
    

**Past continuous** is used for:

- an action that was happening for a while in the past
    
- a longer/background action
    

Form:  
**was/were + verb-ing**

Examples:

- I **was watching** a film.
    
- She **was studying** at 8 o’clock.
    
- They **were playing** football.
    

### When used together

Use **past continuous** for the longer action and **past simple** for the shorter action that happens during it.

- I **was sleeping** when the phone **rang**.
    
- They **were playing** football when it **started** to rain.
    
- We **were baking** a pie when the guests **arrived**.
    

### Quick rule

**Past simple** = what happened?  
**Past continuous** = what was happening?
