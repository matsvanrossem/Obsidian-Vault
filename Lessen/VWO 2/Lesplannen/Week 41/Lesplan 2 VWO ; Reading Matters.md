### Lesson sequence, 2 VWO

| Time | EDI phase | Teacher actions | Student actions | Stepping Stones |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 min | **Lesson start** | Welcome students and display today's objective. | Prepare materials and read the objective. | Reading Matters visible on the board. |
| 3-7 min | **Activate prior knowledge** | Ask students to recall reading strategies from earlier lessons. | Name and explain strategies such as predicting, skimming and scanning. | Reading strategy retrieval. |
| 7-12 min | **Lesson objective** | Explain that students will learn literary terms and use them when reading. | Listen and restate the objective. | Reading Matters. |
| 12-20 min | **Instruction: concept** | Read the literary terms with the class. Explain each term using a simple example. | Read the terms and note meanings/examples. | Read literary terms. |
| 20-27 min | **Instruction: skill/modeling** | Model how to identify one literary term in a short example and explain the evidence. | Follow the model and answer short check questions. | Literary terms. |
| 27-34 min | **Guided practice** | Start ex. 58/59 with the class. Ask students to justify their choices. | Complete selected questions with teacher support. | Start ex. 58/59. |
| 34-40 min | **Independent practice** | Ask students to complete ex. 58/59/60. Support students who need help. | Complete ex. 58/59/60 independently or in pairs. | Do ex. 58/59/60. |
| 40-43 min | **Check for understanding** | Check selected answers and ask for the relevant literary term. | Give answers and explain their choice. | Check ex. 58-60. |
| 43-44 min | **Lesson closure** | Ask students to explain one literary term without looking at the book. | Explain one term in their own words. | Review literary terms. |
| 44-45 min | **Exit ticket/homework** | Ask students to write one literary term and its meaning. Remind them of the week's homework. | Complete the exit ticket and write down homework. | **Homework: Study theme words: the weather and Grammar 2A & 2B.** |
