### Lesson sequence, 2 VWO

| Time      | EDI phase                       | Teacher actions                                                                                     | Student actions                                                  | Stepping Stones                                                   |
| --------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0-3 min   | **Lesson start**                | Welcome students. Establish attention and routines. Display today's lesson objective.               | Prepare materials and read the objective.                        | Chapter/unit visible on board.                                    |
| 3-7 min   | **Activate prior knowledge**    | Ask 3-5 short retrieval questions about the previous lesson.                                        | Answer individually, with mini-whiteboards, orally, or in pairs. | Previous vocabulary/grammar/text.                                 |
| 7-10 min  | **Lesson objective**            | Explain what students are going to learn and why it is useful. Clarify success criteria.            | Restate the objective in their own words.                        | Connect objective to current Stepping Stones lesson.              |
| 10-20 min | **Instruction: concept**        | Explain the new language concept. Model examples and non-examples. Keep explanation in small steps. | Listen, answer questions and take brief notes.                   | Grammar box, vocabulary section, reading/listening strategy, etc. |
| 20-27 min | **Instruction: skill/modeling** | Demonstrate how to complete a Stepping Stones task. Think aloud while solving an example.           | Follow the model and explain individual steps.                   | Complete one example together.                                    |
| 27-34 min | **Guided practice**             | Work through exercises with the class. Check understanding frequently.                              | Complete questions with teacher support.                         | Selected Stepping Stones exercises.                               |
| 34-36 min | **Check for understanding**     | Ask all students questions. Decide whether the class is ready for independent work.                 | Show answers simultaneously or explain reasoning.                | Short exercise/check.                                             |
| 35-45 min | **Independent practice**        | Students practise independently. Provide extended instruction to students who need it.              | Complete Stepping Stones tasks independently.                    | Workbook/coursebook/online task.                                  |
| 45 min    | **Lesson closure**              | Return to the lesson objective. Ask students what they learned.                                     | Demonstrate whether they have achieved the objective.            | One final question based on the lesson.                           |
| 45 min    | **Exit ticket/homework**        | Give a short exit question and explain homework.                                                    | Complete exit ticket and note homework.                          | Homework: __________                                              |
