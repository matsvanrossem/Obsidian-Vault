# Past Simple

De **Past Simple** gebruik je voor iets dat **in het verleden is gebeurd en is afgelopen**. Vaak weet je ook wanneer het gebeurde.

Voorbeelden:

- I played football **yesterday**.
- She visited London **last week**.
- We went to school **this morning**.
- They watched a film **last night**.

Veelvoorkomende tijdsaanduidingen zijn:

- yesterday
- last week
- last year
- last night
- two days ago
- in 2024

### Regelmatige werkwoorden

Bij **regelmatige werkwoorden** maak je de Past Simple meestal met:

**werkwoord + -ed**

| Werkwoord | Past Simple | Voorbeeld                |
| --------- | ----------- | ------------------------ |
| work      | worked      | I **worked** yesterday.  |
| play      | played      | She **played** tennis.   |
| watch     | watched     | We **watched** a film.   |
| clean     | cleaned     | He **cleaned** his room. |
| visit     | visited     | They **visited** London. |

In je LessonUp wordt dezelfde hoofdregel gebruikt: bij regelmatige werkwoorden gebruik je de infinitive + **-ed**.

### Onregelmatige werkwoorden

Bij **onregelmatige werkwoorden** kun je niet gewoon **-ed** toevoegen.

Je gebruikt de **tweede vorm van het werkwoord**.

| Werkwoord | Past Simple | Voorbeeld                 |
| --------- | ----------- | ------------------------- |
| go        | went        | We **went** home.         |
| swim      | swam        | They **swam** in the sea. |
| eat       | ate         | I **ate** a sandwich.     |
| write     | wrote       | She **wrote** a letter.   |
| buy       | bought      | He **bought** a new bike. |
| see       | saw         | We **saw** a film.        |
| do        | did         | I **did** my homework.    |
| have      | had         | She **had** a headache.   |
| come      | came        | They **came** home late.  |
| take      | took        | He **took** the bus.      |

Deze vormen moet je leren. In je LessonUp worden bijvoorbeeld **go / went** en **swim / swam** gebruikt.

### Ontkenningen

Voor een ontkenning gebruik je:

**onderwerp + did not / didn't + hele werkwoord**

Na **didn't** gebruik je dus altijd het **hele werkwoord**.

| Positief              | Negatief                       |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| I played football.    | I **didn't play** football.    |
| She worked yesterday. | She **didn't work** yesterday. |
| They went home.       | They **didn't go** home.       |
| He ate pizza.         | He **didn't eat** pizza.       |
| We saw the film.      | We **didn't see** the film.    |

De regel in je LessonUp is: **subject + did not (didn't) + infinitive**.

### Let op bij onregelmatige werkwoorden

Na **didn't** gebruik je niet de Past Simple-vorm.

Dus:

- I went home.
- I **didn't go** home.

Niet:

- I didn't went home.

Nog een voorbeeld:

- She ate breakfast.
- She **didn't eat** breakfast.

Niet:

- She didn't ate breakfast.

Dit is belangrijk omdat **didn't** al aangeeft dat de zin in het verleden staat.

### Vragen in de Past Simple

Voor een vraag gebruik je:

**Did + onderwerp + hele werkwoord?**

| Zin                    | Vraag                         |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| You played football.   | **Did you play** football?    |
| She worked yesterday.  | **Did she work** yesterday?   |
| They went home.        | **Did they go** home?         |
| He ate pizza.          | **Did he eat** pizza?         |
| Josh did his homework. | **Did Josh do** his homework? |

Ook na **did** gebruik je dus het **hele werkwoord**.

Bijvoorbeeld:

- Did Josh **do** his homework?

Niet:

- Did Josh **did** his homework?

Dit voorbeeld komt ook terug in je LessonUp.

### Past Simple van to be

Het werkwoord **to be** werkt anders.

| Onderwerp | Past Simple |
| --------- | ----------- |
| I         | was         |
| he        | was         |
| she       | was         |
| it        | was         |
| you       | were        |
| we        | were        |
| they      | were        |

Voorbeelden:

- I **was** at home yesterday.
- She **was** tired.
- We **were** at school.
- They **were** at the party.

Voor een ontkenning gebruik je:

- was not = **wasn't**
- were not = **weren't**

Voorbeelden:

- She **wasn't** at school.
- They **weren't** at home.

Voor vragen zet je **was** of **were** vooraan:

- **Was** he at the party?
- **Were** they at school?

Deze vormen staan ook zo in je LessonUp.

# Past Simple

Vul de juiste vorm in.

1. I **____** (play) football yesterday.
2. She **____** (go) to school last Monday.
3. We **____** (watch) a film last night.
4. They **____** (eat) pizza yesterday.
5. He **____** (write) an email.
6. I **____** (not / play) football yesterday.
7. She **____** (not / go) to school.
8. They **____** (not / eat) pizza.
9. **____** you **____** (watch) the film?
10. **____** she **____** (go) home?
11. We **____** (be) at school yesterday.
12. He **____** (be) tired last night.

## Antwoorden

1. **played**
2. **went**
3. **watched**
4. **ate**
5. **wrote**
6. **didn't play**
7. **didn't go**
8. **didn't eat**
9. **Did, watch**
10. **Did, go**
11. **were**
12. **was**
