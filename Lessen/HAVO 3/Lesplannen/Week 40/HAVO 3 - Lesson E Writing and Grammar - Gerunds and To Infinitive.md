### Grammar 2: Gerunds and *to + infinitive*

#### Gerund
A **gerund** is the **verb + -ing** form.

Use a gerund after verbs such as:
- *(dis)like*
- *love*
- *miss*
- *avoid*

Examples:
- *I love driving along the coast.*
- *We miss running in the park.*
- *She avoids making mistakes.*

#### *To + infinitive*
Use **to + infinitive** after verbs such as:
- *hope*
- *ask*
- *refuse*
- *expect*

It can also be used after adjectives that express an opinion.

Examples:
- *I refuse to take the test.*
- *It is nice to see you again.*
- *We hope to go on holiday soon.*
