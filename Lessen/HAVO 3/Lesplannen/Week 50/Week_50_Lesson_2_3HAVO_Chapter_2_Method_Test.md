## Lesson sequence (45 minutes)

| Time | Phase / working method | Teacher actions | Student actions | Book link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 min | **Settle and give instructions** | Seat students and state exact test duration, permitted items, how to ask procedural questions, and submission rules. | Prepare only approved materials; listen to instructions. | Method test |
| 5–9 min | **Distribute and read cover instructions** | Distribute the assessment, confirm pages are complete, remind students to check answer format and conditions. | Read instructions silently and ask only procedural questions. | Method test |
| 9–38 min | **Independent assessment** | Supervise according to school rules. Give neutral timing reminders only. | Complete the test independently and monitor time. | Method test |
| 38–43 min | **Final checking window** | Ask students to check unanswered items, spelling, restrictions and answer format; no content hints. | Reread instructions and finalise work. | Method test |
| 43–45 min | **Collect and close** | Collect and count all papers, follow assessment procedures. | Submit materials quietly. | Method test |
