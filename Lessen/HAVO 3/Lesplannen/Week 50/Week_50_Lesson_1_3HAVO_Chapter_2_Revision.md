## Lesson preparation

| Element | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Lesson topic** | Mixed Chapter 2 revision before the method test |
| **Stepping Stones coverage** | Revise **B Theme Words, Stones 3–4, Grammar 4–6, reading and listening skills**, plus difficult exercises. |
| **Main focus** | Identify weak areas and select targeted test practice while carefully interpreting instructions. |
| **Prior learning** | All planned Chapter 2 instruction through Week 49. |
| **Materials** | Chapter 2 student work, official vocabulary lists, Stones 3–4, Grammar Survey 4–6, mini-whiteboards, revision grid. |
| **Teacher preparation** | Prepare a short diagnostic using verified textbook rules and taught vocabulary. Do not distribute or reveal the actual method test. |

### Learning objectives

1. Assess own readiness across Chapter 2 knowledge and skills.
2. Repair errors through targeted practice and justify the instructions behind each answer.

### Success criteria (student-friendly)

- [ ] I can correctly solve a small mixed diagnostic covering vocabulary, Stones and grammar.
- [ ] I can explain at least one mistake and what caused it.
- [ ] I have a clear personal revision plan for the test.

### Starter on the board

> Project three teacher-made test-style prompts, one each for vocabulary, Stones and the grammar topics verified in the book. Ask students to state the instructions first.

## Lesson sequence (45 minutes)

| Time | Phase / working method | Teacher actions | Student actions | Book link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 min | **Set goal and route** | Explain: diagnose, practise specific gaps, check again. | Open personal notes. | Chapter 2 |
| 4–11 min | **Mixed retrieval diagnostic** | Run 6–8 original short questions spanning vocabulary, Stones, grammar and instructions. | Answer individually without notes. | B / Stones / Grammar |
| 11–15 min | **Interpret results** | Review answers and ask students to label errors: instruction, vocabulary, grammar form or skill. | Choose personal focus area. | Chapter 2 |
| 15–25 min | **Station 1: vocabulary and Stones** | Circulate among pairs using B vocabulary and Stones 3–4 in context. | Translate or explain, then use phrases in short exchanges. | B / Stones 3–4 |
| 25–34 min | **Station 2: grammar and task-reading** | Reteach the most frequent Grammar 4–6 issue to one group; others practise verified rules. | Complete targeted items and explicitly decode instructions. | Grammar 4–6 |
| 34–40 min | **Station 3: receptive skills** | Provide one brief reading or listening strategy check drawn from taught work. | Find evidence for an answer and explain strategy. | Reading / listening |
| 40–43 min | **Post-check** | Repeat two parallel original questions to see if the main errors are repaired. | Respond independently. | Chapter 2 |
| 43–45 min | **Exit plan** | Ask for one strongest topic, one weak topic, and one specific action before the test. | Complete personal revision note. | Test preparation |

## Differentiation

**Additional support:** Create a small teacher-led recap group based on the diagnostic. Offer book-rule reference cards for learning, not for the formal test.

**Challenge / extension:** Write and exchange fair test-style questions with an answer key and instruction analysis.

## Assessment and follow-up

- **Evidence of learning:** Compare initial diagnostic and post-check; collect personal revision targets.
- **Homework / preparation:** Prepare for the method test and revise personally identified weak points.
- **Teacher follow-up:** Note which students need vocabulary, grammar, question-reading or skills support, and plan a short retrieval task for the next lesson.
- **Teacher reflection:** What worked? ____________________ What needs adjustment? ____________________

**Pacing note:** Exercise lists are those in the weekly planner; where the lesson includes more items than fit into 45 minutes, choose representative in-class items and assign the remainder as indicated in the planner. Exact exercise prompts and Grammar Survey rule titles are not provided in the planner.
