| **Lesson topic** | Past continuous and past simple |
| **Stepping Stones section/pages** | Lesson I, exercises 53-56 |
| **Main skill** | Reading / Listening / Speaking / **Writing** / **Grammar** / Vocabulary |
| **Lesson objective** | By the end of the lesson, students can recognise and use the past continuous and past simple to describe actions and events in the past. |
| **Success criteria** | I can: 1. Recognise the past continuous. 2. Explain the difference between the past simple and past continuous in a sentence. 3. Use both tenses correctly in exercises. |
| **Prior knowledge needed** | Past simple, regular and irregular verbs, basic sentence structure. |
| **Key vocabulary / grammar** | Past continuous, past simple, *was/were + verb-ing*. |

| **Homework** | **Study theme words: the weather. Study Grammar 2A & 2B.** |

### Lesson sequence, 2 VWO

| Time | EDI phase | Teacher actions | Student actions | Stepping Stones |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 min | **Lesson start** | Welcome students and display today's objective. | Prepare materials and read the objective. | Lesson I visible on the board. |
| 3-8 min | **Activate prior knowledge** | Review the past simple with several short retrieval questions. | Answer questions and identify past simple forms. | Past simple review. |
| 8-12 min | **Lesson objective** | Explain that students will compare the past continuous and past simple. | Restate the objective in their own words. | Grammar 2A & 2B. |
| 12-17 min | **Instruction: concept** | Let students do ex. 53, then use examples to introduce the past continuous. | Complete ex. 53 and identify patterns. | Do ex. 53. |
| 17-25 min | **Instruction: skill/modeling** | Explain and model the difference between the past continuous and past simple. Use a timeline or example sentences. | Follow the model and explain why each tense is used. | Discuss past continuous and past simple. |
| 25-33 min | **Guided practice** | Work through selected items from ex. 54/55 together. Check understanding after each example. | Complete examples with teacher support and explain their choices. | Start ex. 54/55. |
| 33-40 min | **Independent practice** | Ask students to complete ex. 54/55/56. Give extra instruction where needed. | Complete ex. 54/55/56 independently. | Do ex. 54/55/56. |
| 40-43 min | **Check for understanding** | Check selected answers and ask students to explain which tense they chose and why. | Show answers and explain reasoning. | Check ex. 54-56. |
| 43-44 min | **Lesson closure** | Ask students to explain the difference between “I walked” and “I was walking”. | Give a short explanation. | Review Grammar 2A & 2B. |
| 44-45 min | **Exit ticket/homework** | Give one sentence in which students choose the correct tense. Remind them of the homework. | Complete the exit ticket and write down homework. | **Homework: Study theme words: the weather. Study Grammar 2A & 2B.** |
