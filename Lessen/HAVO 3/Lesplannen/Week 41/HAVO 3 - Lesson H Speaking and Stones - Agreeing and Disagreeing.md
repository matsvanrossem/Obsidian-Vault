### Lesson sequence, 3 HAVO

| Time | EDI phase | Teacher actions | Student actions | Stepping Stones |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 min | **Lesson start** | Welcome students, establish attention and display today's lesson objective. | Prepare materials and read the objective. | Lesson H visible on the board. |
| 3-8 min | **Activate prior knowledge** | Ask students how they would agree or disagree with a classmate in English. Collect a few examples. | Give phrases they already know. | Prior knowledge check. |
| 8-14 min | **Instruction: Stone 2** | Read Stone 2: *Agreeing and disagreeing with someone*. Highlight useful sentence patterns and pronunciation. | Read along and identify useful phrases. | Read Stone 2. |
| 14-19 min | **Vocabulary instruction** | Read and discuss the theme words: Expressing (dis)agreement. Model how they fit into short responses. | Take notes and practise selected phrases. | Theme words. |
| 19-25 min | **Guided practice** | Complete ex. 45 and 46. Check how the phrases are used in context. | Complete ex. 45/46 and compare answers. | Do ex. 45/46. |
| 25-35 min | **Speaking practice** | Set up paired speaking practice based on the lesson topic. Encourage students to use Stone 2 rather than only saying *yes* or *no*. | Practise agreeing and disagreeing in pairs. | Preparation for ex. 48/50. |
| 35-40 min | **Independent practice** | Ask students to complete ex. 48 and 50. Monitor language use and support where needed. | Complete ex. 48/50. | Do ex. 48/50. |
| 40-43 min | **Check for understanding** | Ask several pairs to demonstrate a short exchange. Give brief feedback on correct use of the phrases. | Perform a short dialogue. | Speaking check. |
| 43-44 min | **Lesson closure** | Ask students to name one phrase for agreeing and one for disagreeing. | Give examples from Stone 2. | Review Stone 2. |
| 44-45 min | **Exit ticket/homework** | Ask students to write or say one polite disagreement phrase. Remind them of the homework. | Complete the exit ticket and write down homework. | **Homework: Study Stone 2 (p. 78).** |
