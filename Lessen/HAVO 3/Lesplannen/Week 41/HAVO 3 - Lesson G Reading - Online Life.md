### Lesson sequence, 3 HAVO

| Time | EDI phase | Teacher actions | Student actions | Stepping Stones |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 min | **Lesson start** | Welcome students, establish attention and display today's lesson objective. | Prepare materials and read the objective. | Lesson G visible on the board. |
| 3-8 min | **Activate prior knowledge** | Discuss words G. Ask students to explain or infer meanings and use selected words in a sentence. | Recall meanings and give examples. | Discuss words G. |
| 8-13 min | **Pre-reading** | Introduce the topic of teenagers and their online lives. Ask students to predict what the text may discuss. | Share predictions and activate background knowledge. | Preparation for reading. |
| 13-18 min | **Guided practice** | Complete ex. 40 (p. 62) and check the approach together. | Complete ex. 40 and explain answers. | Do ex. 40 (p. 62). |
| 18-28 min | **Reading** | Guide students through *When teenagers realize their whole life is already online*. Remind them to skim for the main idea and scan for details. | Read the text and mark key information. | Read the text. |
| 28-38 min | **Independent practice** | Ask students to complete ex. 41-44. Support students who need help locating evidence in the text. | Complete ex. 41-44 independently or in pairs. | Do ex. 41-44. |
| 38-42 min | **Check for understanding** | Check selected answers and ask students to point to evidence from the text. | Give answers and justify them with evidence. | Check ex. 41-44. |
| 42-44 min | **Lesson closure** | Return to the objective. Ask students to summarise the text in one or two sentences. | Give a short summary. | Review. |
| 44-45 min | **Exit ticket/homework** | Ask for one word from G and its meaning. Remind students of the homework. | Complete the exit ticket and write down homework. | **Homework: Study words G (p. 77).** |
