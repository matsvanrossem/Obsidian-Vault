### Lesson sequence, 3 HAVO

| Time | EDI phase | Teacher actions | Student actions | Stepping Stones |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 min | **Lesson start** | Welcome students, establish attention and display today's lesson objective. | Prepare materials and read the objective. | Lesson I visible on the board. |
| 3-8 min | **Activate prior knowledge** | Ask students what changes when a statement becomes a question or a negative sentence. Use simple examples. | Identify differences in word order and auxiliary verbs. | Prior knowledge check. |
| 8-13 min | **Guided start** | Complete ex. 51 (p. 70) and use it to introduce the lesson focus. | Complete ex. 51 and discuss answers. | Do ex. 51 (p. 70). |
| 13-20 min | **Instruction: concept** | Discuss Grammar 3: questions and negations. Model the sentence structure given in the coursebook and highlight auxiliary verbs and word order. | Listen, take notes and transform example sentences. | Grammar 3. |
| 20-25 min | **Modeling** | Think aloud while changing statements into questions and negations. Ask students to explain each step. | Follow the examples and explain the rule. | Grammar 3 examples. |
| 25-35 min | **Independent practice** | Ask students to complete ex. 51/52/53/54/55 as listed in the lesson planning. Provide extra instruction where needed. | Complete the exercises independently or in pairs. | Do ex. 51/52/53/54/55. |
| 35-40 min | **Check for understanding** | Check selected answers and ask students to explain the word order and auxiliary verb used. | Give answers and justify their choices. | Check exercises. |
| 40-43 min | **Writing check** | Give two short statements and ask students to write one question and one negative sentence from each. | Write the transformed sentences. | Grammar application. |
| 43-44 min | **Lesson closure** | Return to the objective. Ask students to explain one rule for questions and one rule for negations. | Summarise the grammar rule. | Review Grammar 3. |
| 44-45 min | **Exit ticket/homework** | Give one final sentence to transform. Remind students of the homework. | Complete the exit ticket and write down homework. | **Homework: Study Grammar 3 (p. 79).** |
