### Lesson sequence, 2 VWO

| Time | EDI phase | Teacher actions | Student actions | Stepping Stones |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 min | **Lesson start** | Welcome students and display today's review objective. | Prepare materials and read the objective. | Theme 1 review visible on the board. |
| 3-8 min | **Activate prior knowledge** | Ask several retrieval questions about vocabulary and grammar from Theme 1. | Answer individually and identify topics they remember well or find difficult. | Theme 1 retrieval. |
| 8-11 min | **Lesson objective** | Explain how Test Yourself, Grammar rehearsal and Self study will help students identify what they need to practise. | Listen and choose one personal focus area. | Review structure. |
| 11-20 min | **Guided practice** | Start Test Yourself. Model how to check an answer and correct a mistake rather than only marking it wrong. | Complete review questions and correct mistakes. | Test Yourself. |
| 20-29 min | **Independent practice** | Students continue Test Yourself independently. Circulate and note common difficulties. | Work independently and check answers. | Test Yourself. |
| 29-37 min | **Grammar rehearsal** | Review common difficulties, especially past simple and past continuous. Direct students to suitable grammar rehearsal tasks. | Complete grammar rehearsal and ask questions where needed. | Grammar rehearsal. |
| 37-41 min | **Self study** | Ask students to choose a self-study task based on what they still need to practise. | Work on a personally relevant review task. | Self study. |
| 41-44 min | **Check for understanding** | Ask students to identify one thing they can now do and one thing they still need to study. | Reflect on their progress. | Theme 1 review. |
| 44-45 min | **Exit ticket/homework** | Ask students to write down their main study focus. Remind them of the week's homework. | Complete the exit ticket and write down homework. | **Homework: Study theme words: the weather. Study Grammar 2A & 2B.** |
