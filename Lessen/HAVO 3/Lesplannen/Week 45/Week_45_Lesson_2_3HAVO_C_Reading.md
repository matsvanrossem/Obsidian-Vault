## Lesson preparation

| Element | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Lesson topic** | C Reading and comprehension strategies |

| **Main focus** | Read for overall meaning and detail while understanding exactly what each question requires. |

| **Teacher preparation** | Preview the actual reading passages and exercise instructions; choose one short passage for live modelling. |

### Learning objectives

1. Use the question to decide what to look for in a reading text.
2. Support answers with words or details from the passage.

### Success criteria (student-friendly)

- [ ] I can find the command word and relevant keywords in a question.
- [ ] I can locate evidence instead of relying on guesses.
- [ ] I can check that the answer format matches the task.

### Starter on the board

> Show two invented prompts: “What is the main point?” and “Give TWO details.” Ask why the answers must look different.

## Lesson sequence (45 minutes)

| Time | Phase / working method | Teacher actions | Student actions | Book link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 min | **Goal and recap** | Explain that reading the questions is a separate skill from understanding the passage. | Get text and pen ready. | C Reading |
| 4–9 min | **Question type warm-up** | Use two sample prompts to distinguish main point, detail and number of requested answers. | Mark task words and special conditions. | Reading strategy |
| 9–15 min | **Teacher modelling** | Read the actual instruction for exercise 17, mark TASK, skim text for topic, and locate evidence for one sample answer. | Follow along and explain the chosen evidence. | 17 |
| 15–24 min | **Pairs: reading detectives** | Assign 18a–18d with rotating roles: Question Decoder and Evidence Finder. | Read, identify expected answer and highlight relevant lines. | 18a–18d |
| 24–30 min | **Discuss and correct** | Collect answers requiring explanation, ask which part of the text supports each answer. | Compare evidence and revise answers. | 18a–18d |
| 30–39 min | **Independent practice** | Set 19–21. Direct learners to mark every instruction before writing. | Work independently and note difficult questions. | 19–21 |
| 39–43 min | **Wrong-answer clinic** | Show a teacher-made incomplete answer missing one requested detail and ask pupils to repair it. | Explain why it is incomplete. | Reading strategy |
| 43–45 min | **Exit ticket** | Ask students to finish: “Before I answer a reading question, I will...” | Write one concrete habit. | C Reading |

## Differentiation

**Additional support:** Break the text into short sections and supply paragraph numbers or a keyword bank when the book allows.

**Challenge / extension:** Require short quoted evidence or paraphrase plus explanation for each answer.

## Assessment and follow-up

- **Evidence of learning:** Sample evidence-marked answers and note whether errors come from locating information or misreading the question.
- **Homework / preparation:** Review C vocabulary and prepare Stone 3.
- **Teacher follow-up:** Note which students need vocabulary, grammar, question-reading or skills support, and plan a short retrieval task for the next lesson.
- **Teacher reflection:** What worked? ____________________ What needs adjustment? ____________________
