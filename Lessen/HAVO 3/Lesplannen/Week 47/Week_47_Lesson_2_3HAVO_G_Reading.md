### Learning objectives

1. Identify the expected answer type and keywords in a reading question.
2. Find supporting evidence in a passage and paraphrase it as needed.

### Success criteria (student-friendly)

- [ ] I can tell the difference between a main-idea question and a detail question.
- [ ] I can identify the relevant sentence or paragraph.
- [ ] I can meet conditions such as giving a specified number of reasons.

### Starter on the board

> Ask: Why is answering with one reason wrong when the instruction says “Give two reasons”, even if the reason is correct?

## Lesson sequence (45 minutes)

| Time | Phase / working method | Teacher actions | Student actions | Book link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 min | **Start** | State the lesson goal: answer the question asked and show the evidence. | Open G Reading. | G Reading |
| 4–9 min | **Retrieval from C Reading** | Ask students to mark instruction words in three teacher-made sample prompts. | Mark task and special conditions. | Reading skills |
| 9–15 min | **Model reading steps** | Use exercise 40 to demonstrate scanning text structure and locating evidence for a response. | Annotate the relevant lines. | 40 |
| 15–24 min | **Guided pairs** | Assign 41a–41d; pairs rotate Decoder and Evidence Checker roles. | Read questions, find evidence and draft answers. | 41a–41d |
| 24–30 min | **Discuss evidence** | Compare selected answers, checking content and required form separately. | Defend and revise responses. | 41a–41d |
| 30–39 min | **Independent reading** | Set 42–44 and circulate. Prompt students to underline conditions before solving. | Complete tasks independently. | 42–44 |
| 39–43 min | **Mistake analysis** | Compare a correct, supported answer and an incomplete answer to a teacher-made example. | Name what is missing. | Reading skills |
| 43–45 min | **Exit ticket** | Have students write a two-line instruction-reading checklist. | Submit checklist. | G Reading |

## Differentiation

**Additional support:** Offer paragraph prompts, allow brief English keywords and help students split multi-part instructions.

**Challenge / extension:** Demand concise paraphrased answers rather than copied chunks, with evidence indicated.

## Assessment and follow-up

- **Evidence of learning:** Collect one evidence-marked response and note task-reading versus comprehension problems.
- **Homework / preparation:** Finish G Reading; review B vocabulary and Grammar 4–5.
- **Teacher follow-up:** Note which students need vocabulary, grammar, question-reading or skills support, and plan a short retrieval task for the next lesson.
- **Teacher reflection:** What worked? ____________________ What needs adjustment? ____________________
