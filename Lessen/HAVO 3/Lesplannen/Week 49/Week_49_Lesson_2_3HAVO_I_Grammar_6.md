### Learning objectives

1. Recognise and apply the form taught in Grammar 6.
2. Read grammar instructions carefully and check both grammar and meaning.

### Success criteria (student-friendly)

- [ ] I can explain the key rule using a verified coursebook example.
- [ ] I can apply the rule in relevant workbook items.
- [ ] I can revise a sentence after a specific correction.

### Starter on the board

> Display a book-verified sentence illustrating Grammar 6 and ask students what the form contributes to the meaning.

## Lesson sequence (45 minutes)

| Time | Phase / working method | Teacher actions | Student actions | Book link |

## Differentiation

**Additional support:** Provide official rule text, partially completed examples and targeted feedback before independent work.

**Challenge / extension:** Ask learners to contrast Grammar 6 with one earlier rule and explain why one form is preferable in context.

## Assessment and follow-up

- **Evidence of learning:** Use short concept check, exercise samples and the final sentence to distinguish rule knowledge from task-reading.
- **Homework / preparation:** Study Grammar 6. Revise Grammar 4–6, Stones 3–4 and Theme Words; finish assigned exercises.
- **Teacher follow-up:** Note which students need vocabulary, grammar, question-reading or skills support, and plan a short retrieval task for the next lesson.
- **Teacher reflection:** What worked? ____________________ What needs adjustment? ____________________
