### Learning objectives

1. Recognise and apply Grammar 4 and Grammar 5 as defined in the Grammar Survey.
2. Read grammar instructions for the required form before completing sentences.

### Success criteria (student-friendly)

- [ ] I can explain when to use each of the two rules using the book examples.
- [ ] I can choose the requested form in an exercise.
- [ ] I can find and correct one form error using a rule.

### Starter on the board

> Put two short example sentences from the actual Grammar Survey on the board; students identify what changes and why.

## Lesson sequence (45 minutes)

| Time | Phase / working method | Teacher actions | Student actions | Book link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 min | **Start and goals** | Display the names of Grammar 4 and 5 exactly as in the book. | Open the Grammar Survey. | E Grammar |
| 4–9 min | **Prior-knowledge check** | Show two verified book examples, ask which pattern students recognise and what the instruction demands. | Choose and explain. | Grammar 4 and 5 |
| 9–17 min | **Explicit instruction** | Teach Grammar 4 using the book rule and a correct worked example; check understanding. | Explain the rule in their own words. | 27a–27c |
| 17–24 min | **Explicit instruction** | Teach Grammar 5 using the book rule and contrast with Grammar 4 where relevant. | Annotate the different patterns. | 28a–28b, 29a–29b |
| 24–33 min | **Guided work: decode first** | Use selected tasks from 27a–29b. Students highlight instructions including tense/form/word limits before answering. | Complete representative items and verbalise the requested form. | 27a–29b |
| 33–40 min | **Independent application** | Assign exercises 30a–30b and 31 and any remaining priority items. Circulate and support. | Work independently, then check one answer with a peer. | 30a–30b, 31 |
| 40–43 min | **Error clinic** | Present a common error from anonymous work or a verified teacher-made example. | Find the error and explain the rule. | Grammar 4 and 5 |
| 43–45 min | **Exit ticket** | Ask for one example sentence for each rule and a matching explanation. | Write or dictate two short forms. | E Grammar |

## Differentiation

**Additional support:** Provide the official book rule examples and highlight what the instruction asks for. Group students needing reteaching at a side table.

**Challenge / extension:** Have pupils create one misleading but fair grammar question per rule with an answer key.

## Assessment and follow-up

- **Evidence of learning:** Separate rule mistakes from misunderstandings of instruction language in exercises and exit tickets.
- **Homework / preparation:** Study Grammar 4 and Grammar 5 and finish the remaining E exercises.
- **Teacher follow-up:** Note which students need vocabulary, grammar, question-reading or skills support, and plan a short retrieval task for the next lesson.
- **Teacher reflection:** What worked? ____________________ What needs adjustment? ____________________

**Pacing note:** Exercise lists are those in the weekly planner; where the lesson includes more items than fit into 45 minutes, choose representative in-class items and assign the remainder as indicated in the planner. Exact exercise prompts and Grammar Survey rule titles are not provided in the planner.
