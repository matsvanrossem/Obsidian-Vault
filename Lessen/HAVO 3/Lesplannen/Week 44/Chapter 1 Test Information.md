## **Homework: Chapter 1 – The world around you**

- **Study Grammar 1: Compounds with *some-, any-, every-, no-***
  - *-thing* for things
  - *-body / -one* for people
  - *-where* for places

- **Study Grammar 2: Gerunds and *to + infinitive***
  - gerund = **verb + -ing**
  - *to + infinitive* = **to + base verb**

- **Study Grammar 3: Questions and negations in the past and present**
  - with **to be / auxiliary verbs**: change the word order for questions; add **not** for negations
  - with other main verbs: use **do / does / did**
  - after **do / does / did**, use the **infinitive**
