### Learning objectives

1. Apply a before/during/after watching strategy to Chapter 2 material.
2. Recognise whether a question asks for an overall idea, a specific detail or an inference.

### Success criteria (student-friendly)

- [ ] I can restate a watching question in my own words.
- [ ] I can note a relevant clue while watching.
- [ ] I can explain why a selected answer fits the question.

### Starter on the board

> Show three invented prompts: “What is the clip mostly about?”, “Where does the event happen?” and “Why might the person react?” Ask what kind of information each needs.

## Differentiation

**Additional support:** Replay short sections with a guiding prompt, and allow brief keywords rather than full notes during viewing.

**Challenge / extension:** Ask for a second plausible interpretation and the evidence that rules it out.

## Assessment and follow-up
