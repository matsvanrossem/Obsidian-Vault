## Lesson preparation

| Element | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Lesson topic** | Repair listening strategies and complete earlier chapter work |
| **Stepping Stones coverage** | Revisit **F Listening 35–39**; catch up on any unfinished **A–G**. |
| **Main focus** | Diagnose whether errors come from task understanding, comprehension or missing subject knowledge; improve one difficulty. |

| **Materials** | Audio from F Listening, original student work, revision checklist, task-tracking grid, headphones if available. |

### Learning objectives

1. Apply an effective listening repair strategy to a difficult item.
2. Use an individual catch-up plan to complete and correct priority chapter work.

### Success criteria (student-friendly)

- [ ] I can name one specific listening obstacle.
- [ ] I can improve at least two previously uncertain responses using evidence.
- [ ] I can list the exercises I still need to finish.

### Starter on the board

> Ask pupils to rate their difficulty with gist, details, pace and task instructions, then select one to address.

## Lesson sequence (45 minutes)

| Time | Phase / working method | Teacher actions | Student actions | Book link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 min | **Goals and triage** | Display two lesson routes: listening repair and unfinished-work completion. | Choose one specific target. | F Listening / A–G |
| 4–10 min | **Diagnose mistakes** | Show common error categories: missed instruction, misunderstood word, missed audio clue. | Label own two difficult answers. | 35–39 |
| 10–17 min | **Model a replay strategy** | Choose one hard F item; mark needed information, replay, check evidence and revise. | Follow and annotate the process. | F 35–39 |
| 17–28 min | **Listening repair station** | Students replay or analyse selected F items in pairs or with teacher. | Correct difficult listening items with brief evidence notes. | F 35–39 |
| 28–38 min | **Catch-up workshop** | Use a checklist of A–G work; support students with the largest gaps first. | Finish priority missed exercises or review incorrect answers. | A–G |
| 38–43 min | **Accountability check** | Ask each student to show one corrected response and updated completion list. | Share evidence of improvement. | F / A–G |
| 43–45 min | **Exit ticket** | Students write their next action and the cause of a previous error. | Submit individual next step. | Revision |

## Differentiation

**Additional support:** Provide one replay focus at a time; give a priority order for unfinished work and mini-teacher conferences.

**Challenge / extension:** Have pupils write a short explanation of why a plausible distractor was wrong, then support a peer without giving the answer.

## Assessment and follow-up

- **Evidence of learning:** Collect a before/after correction and individual completion tracker.
- **Homework / preparation:** Complete unfinished assignments and note difficult topics for revision.
- **Teacher follow-up:** Note which students need vocabulary, grammar, question-reading or skills support, and plan a short retrieval task for the next lesson.
- **Teacher reflection:** What worked? ____________________ What needs adjustment? ____________________

Exact exercise prompts and Grammar Survey rule titles are not provided in the planner.
