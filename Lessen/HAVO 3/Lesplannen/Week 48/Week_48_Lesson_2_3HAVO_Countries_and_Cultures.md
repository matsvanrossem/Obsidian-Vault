### Learning objectives

1. Extract accurate information from the assigned Countries & Cultures material.
2. Explain an answer clearly and distinguish information in the source from opinion.

### Success criteria (student-friendly)

- [ ] I can identify the task in each exercise.
- [ ] I can give a relevant fact from the coursebook material.
- [ ] I can point to the evidence supporting my answer.

### Starter on the board

> Ask: What is the difference between “Find a fact from the text” and “Give your opinion”? Discuss without needing a specific country.

## Lesson sequence (45 minutes)

| Time | Phase / working method | Teacher actions | Student actions | Book link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 min | **Welcome and goals** | Name the Countries & Cultures focus from the actual coursebook. | Open the relevant pages. | Countries & Cultures |
| 4–9 min | **Fact versus opinion warm-up** | Give two teacher-made statements and ask which needs evidence in a text. | Discuss and label fact/opinion. | Reading skill |

| 15–25 min | **Guided text exploration** | Read or view the actual Culture material and lead key items in 32a–32e. | Extract information; write brief evidence notes. | 32a–32e |
| 25–34 min | **Partner work** | Set 33–34. Invite pairs to explain why the source supports each response. | Work together, check answers and revise. | 33–34 |
| 34–40 min | **Independent completion** | Allow time to finish assigned Culture tasks or targeted A–G reading work. | Complete priority tasks independently. | Countries & Cultures / catch-up |

| 43–45 min | **Exit ticket** | Ask what the source actually stated and which question was hardest to interpret. | Write a fact and a question-reading reflection. | Countries & Cultures |

## Differentiation

**Additional support:** Break long passages into short sections and provide question stems: “According to the text...”.

**Challenge / extension:** Compare a factual claim and an interpretation; ask pupils to justify the difference with evidence.

## Assessment and follow-up

- **Evidence of learning:** Check source-grounded facts and whether students have completed 32–34 as assigned.
- **Homework / preparation:** Review Theme Words, Stone 3 and Grammar 4–5.
- **Teacher follow-up:** Note which students need vocabulary, grammar, question-reading or skills support, and plan a short retrieval task for the next lesson.
- **Teacher reflection:** What worked? ____________________ What needs adjustment? ____________________
