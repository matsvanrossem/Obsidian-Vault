### EDI lesson sequence, 3 HAVO

|Time|EDI phase|Teacher actions|Student actions|Stepping Stones|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|0-3 min|**Lesson start**|Establish attention. Show the lesson objective and what students need.|Prepare books/materials and read objective.|Open correct unit/page.|
|3-8 min|**Activate prior knowledge**|Ask 3-5 short retrieval questions from the previous lesson.|Answer individually, orally, on paper, or mini-whiteboards.|Previous vocabulary/grammar.|
|8-11 min|**Lesson objective**|Explain what students will learn and what a successful answer/product looks like.|Listen and identify the goal.|Connect objective to current section.|
|11-19 min|**Explicit instruction**|Explain the new concept in small steps. Use simple examples and non-examples.|Listen, answer questions, complete short checks.|Grammar box, vocabulary, reading strategy, etc.|
|19-25 min|**Modeling: I do**|Demonstrate one Stepping Stones task. Think aloud and show the steps.|Observe and answer teacher questions.|One example exercise.|
|25-32 min|**Guided practice: We do**|Complete several examples together. Give prompts and gradually reduce support.|Answer, explain choices, correct mistakes.|Selected exercises.|
|32-35 min|**Check for understanding**|Check the whole class before moving on.|Give individual answers.|Short diagnostic question.|
|35-44 min|**Independent practice: You do**|Students work independently. Teacher supports students who need extra instruction.|Complete selected exercises independently.|Workbook/coursebook/online exercises.|
|44-48 min|**Review**|Discuss common errors and revisit the lesson objective.|Correct work and explain one answer.|Review one or two exercises.|
|48-50 min|**Exit ticket**|Give one short task directly connected to the objective.|Complete exit ticket individually.|Homework if appropriate.|
