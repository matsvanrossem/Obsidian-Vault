## Assignment 1: Sociogram

**Deadline: Check Brightspace**

The course schedule places this assessment after Workshop 3.

### Purpose

Analyse the social relationships within one of your classes and develop a strategy to improve the classroom's social dynamics.

### Checklist

**Preparation**

-  Select a class you teach.
    
-  Study relevant literature about classroom relationships.
    
-  Determine how to investigate relationships between students.
    
-  Follow your school's privacy requirements.
    

**Create the Sociogram**

-  Collect information about students' social relationships.
    
-  Create a sociogram representing relationships within the class.
    
-  Identify important social patterns.
    
-  Identify groups and possible isolated students.
    
-  Examine the overall classroom social structure.
    

**Analysis**

-  Analyse the relationships and group dynamics.
    
-  Identify relevant strengths and challenges in the classroom.
    
-  Connect your findings to pedagogical theory.
    

**Action Plan**

-  Develop a plan for positively influencing classroom relationships.
    
-  Describe specific actions you could take as a teacher.
    
-  Explain why these actions might be effective.
    
-  Reflect on your own pedagogical role.
    
-  Submit your completed assignment through Brightspace.
    

**Goal:** Demonstrate that you understand classroom social dynamics and can respond to them pedagogically.

## Assignment 2: Pedagogical Essay

**Opdracht 2: Pedagogisch handelen**

**Deadline: Check Brightspace**
### Purpose
Investigate a pedagogical issue from your own teaching practice, using interviews and educational literature.

### Step 1: Select a Topic

Choose a topic connected to one of the final three course themes:

- Adolescent development.
    
- Cultural diversity and students' social environments.
    
- Citizenship education.
    

### Step 2: Formulate a Research Question

-  Identify a pedagogical issue from your teaching practice.
    
-  Formulate a clear question about this issue.
    
-  Explain why the issue matters.
    
-  Connect the question to your own teaching experiences.
    

### Step 3: Conduct Interviews

-  Determine who can provide relevant perspectives.
    
-  Prepare interview questions.
    
-  Conduct interviews about your pedagogical issue.
    
-  Document the relevant findings.
    
-  Identify recurring themes and different perspectives.
    

### Step 4: Literature Review

-  Find relevant academic literature.
    
-  Identify theories that help explain your issue.
    
-  Connect the literature to your interview findings.
    
-  Analyse the issue using both theory and practical evidence.
    

### Step 5: Write the Essay

-  Introduce your pedagogical question.
    
-  Explain the practical context.
    
-  Present your interview findings.
    
-  Analyse them using academic literature.
    
-  Explain what the findings mean for teaching.
    
-  Formulate recommendations for yourself and/or the school.
    
-  Reflect on your own pedagogical development.
    
-  Submit through Brightspace.
    

**Important:** The manual does not specify a word count for this essay. Check the assignment instructions in Brightspace.
