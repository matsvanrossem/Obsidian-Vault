



## 1. Set Up Your Portfolio

- [ ]  Open the **eJournal** portfolio template in Brightspace.
    
- [ ]  Organize your portfolio according to the **12 tasks**.
    
- [ ]  Check the submission deadline in Brightspace.
    
- [ ]  Collect evidence of your teaching activities.
    
- [ ]  For each task, briefly explain:
    
    - [ ] Where and when you collected the evidence.
        
    - [ ] Why you selected it.
        
    - [ ] What it demonstrates about your teaching abilities.
        

---

## 2. PiP1a Checklist

### Lesson Planning (Tasks 1 to 5)

- [ ]  Create **2 lesson plans**.
    
- [ ]  Reflect on how both lessons went.
    
- [ ]  Explain whether the learning objectives were achieved.
    
- [ ]  Explain how you evaluated students' learning.
    

### Teaching & Classroom Management (Tasks 4 and 7)

- [ ]  Record the beginning of one lesson (task 4).
    
- [ ]  Add an explanation of the video.
    
- [ ]  Get a lesson observation from your workplace supervisor (_werkplekbegeleider_).
    
- [ ]  Write a reflection on their feedback.
    
- [ ]  Include a video showing your interaction with students (task 7).
    

### Professional Development (Task 12)

- [ ]  Obtain your final assessment from your workplace supervisor.
    
- [ ]  Complete your self-assessment using the rubric.
    
- [ ]  Write a reflection report of **1 to 2 A4 pages**.
    

**Required level for PiP1a:**

- Tasks 1 to 5 and 7: minimum Level 1.
    
- Task 12: minimum Level 2.
    

---

## 3. PiP1b Checklist

### Lesson Planning (Tasks 1 to 5)

- [ ]  Prepare **3 consecutive lesson plans**.
    
- [ ]  Reflect on the implementation of all three lessons.
    
- [ ]  Explain how you incorporated previous feedback.
    
- [ ]  Evaluate whether your learning objectives were achieved.
    
- [ ]  Explain how your teaching activities contributed to learning.
    

### Teaching Practice (Task 4)

- [ ]  Record **2 video clips** demonstrating varied and active teaching methods.
    
- [ ]  Obtain **3 lesson observations** from your workplace supervisor.
    
- [ ]  Write a reflection on each observation.
    
- [ ]  Include the observation report from your university supervisor (_instituutsopleider_).
    
- [ ]  Reflect on that observation report.
    

### Assessment (Task 5)

- [ ]  Include examples of formative and/or summative assessment.
    
- [ ]  Explain what the assessment results revealed.
    
- [ ]  Describe how you used or will use these results to improve your teaching.
    

### Student Relationships (Task 6)

- [ ]  Complete the **VIL questionnaire** in 2 classes.
    
- [ ]  Complete the VIL analysis assignment.
    

### Classroom Management (Task 7)

- [ ]  Include **2 video clips** showing interactions with students.
    

### Student Development (Task 8)

- [ ]  Collect reports or audio/video evidence of classroom or individual student conversations.
    
- [ ]  Demonstrate how you support students' personal development.
    

### Pedagogical Vision (Task 9)

- [ ]  Explain your personal pedagogical vision.
    
- [ ]  Critically connect your vision to the evidence collected for Task 8.
    
- [ ]  Use relevant material from Pedagogiek 1, Assignment 2.
    

### School Participation (Task 10)

- [ ]  Participate in activities beyond your own lessons.
    
- [ ]  Document your involvement, for example:
    
    - [ ] Meetings.
        
    - [ ] Parent-teacher conversations.
        
    - [ ] School activities.
        
    - [ ] Department meetings.
        

### Collaboration (Task 11)

- [ ]  Obtain feedback from **2 people at school other than your workplace supervisor**.
    
- [ ]  Make sure their feedback addresses your professional collaboration.
    

### Professional Development (Task 12)

- [ ]  Obtain the final PiP1b assessment from your workplace supervisor.
    
- [ ]  Complete your self-assessment.
    
- [ ]  Write your reflection report.
    

**Required level for PiP1b: All 12 tasks must be at least Level 2.**

---

## 4. Reflection Report (Task 12)

For both PiP1a and PiP1b, write approximately **1 to 2 A4 pages**.

Your report must cover three areas.

**A. Your Development**

- [ ]  Describe how you developed as a teacher.
    
- [ ]  Discuss your progress on the 12 tasks.
    
- [ ]  Identify your strengths.
    
- [ ]  Identify areas for improvement.
    

**B. Reflection**

- [ ]  Explain how you learned from teaching experience.
    
- [ ]  Discuss feedback from supervisors.
    
- [ ]  Explain how educational theory connects to your classroom practice.
    
- [ ]  Give examples of how you applied theory.
    

**C. Future Goals**

- [ ]  Formulate **2 concrete learning goals**.
    
- [ ]  Explain how you plan to achieve them.
    
- [ ]  Explain how these goals connect to the teacher you want to become.
    

---

## 5. Video Recording & Privacy

- [ ]  Ask your workplace supervisor for permission by email before recording.
    
- [ ]  Use your university email address.
    
- [ ]  Use school recording equipment where possible.
    
- [ ]  Avoid showing identifiable students without the necessary permission.
    
- [ ]  Make sure recordings have clear audio and video.
    
- [ ]  Upload personal data securely within **1 week**.
    
- [ ]  Delete recordings from the recording device within **1 week**.
    
- [ ]  Anonymize student information.
    
- [ ]  Only share student information with authorized supervisors.
    

**Important:** The guide specifies a maximum of 4 minutes for video fragments and states that fragments should come from different lessons. However, the PiP1a instructions also mention recording the first 10 minutes of a lesson. Check this discrepancy with your supervisor.

---

## 6. Final Submission

Before submitting:

- [ ]  Check that all required tasks are completed.
    
- [ ]  Check that every task includes the necessary evidence.
    
- [ ]  Add explanations and reflections to your evidence.
    
- [ ]  Complete your self-assessment.
    
- [ ]  Make sure your workplace supervisor has published their final assessment in eJournal.
    
- [ ]  Check that your reflection report is included.
    
- [ ]  Confirm that the portfolio follows the required structure.
    
- [ ]  Submit through **eJournal: "Collectie ter beoordeling indienen"**.
    
- [ ]  Attend your final assessment meeting with your university supervisor.
    

**Important:** An incomplete portfolio will not be assessed.
