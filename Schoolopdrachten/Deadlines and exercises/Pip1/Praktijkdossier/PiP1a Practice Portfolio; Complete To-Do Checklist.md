# 2. Tasks 1 to 5: Lesson Planning and Teaching

## Tasks 1, 2 and 3: Lesson Plans

These three tasks use the same evidence.

### Required: 2 lesson plans with reflections

-  Select two lessons you have taught.
    
-  Prepare a lesson plan for each.
    
-  Include clear learning objectives.
    
-  Describe students' prior knowledge.
    
-  Explain your choice of teaching activities.
    
-  Describe the teaching materials you used.
    
-  Include lesson timing and structure.
    
-  Explain how you planned to support students.
    
-  Explain how you assessed whether learning objectives were achieved.
    

### Reflection

For both lessons:

-  Describe how the lesson went.
    
-  Explain whether the learning objectives were achieved.
    
-  Evaluate how effective your teaching activities were.
    
-  Reflect on your classroom management.
    
-  Identify improvements for future lessons.
    

### eJournal

-  Upload evidence for Task 1.
    
-  Upload evidence for Task 2.
    
-  Upload evidence for Task 3.
    
-  Add a short explanation for each task.
    
-  Click **Indienen / Submit** for each individual task.
    

**Tip:** You can use your lesson plan from Vakdidactiek 1.

## Task 4: Teaching Activities

### Required: Teaching video and lesson observation

**Video**

-  Ask your workplace supervisor for permission to record.
    
-  Record the beginning of a lesson.
    
-  Select the required video evidence.
    
-  Upload the video to the UU video platform.
    
-  Add the video to eJournal.
    
-  Explain what the recording demonstrates about your teaching.
    

**Supervisor Observation**

-  Arrange a lesson observation with your workplace supervisor.
    
-  Obtain their observation report.
    
-  Write a reflection on their feedback.
    
-  Upload the observation report.
    
-  Upload your reflection.
    
-  Submit Task 4.
    

**Important:** The PiP1a overview asks for the first ten minutes of a lesson, while the general PiP1 manual specifies video fragments of a maximum of four minutes. Ask your university supervisor which length you should submit.

# 4. Task 12: Professional Development

Task 12 consists of three main components.

## A. Final Assessment From Your Supervisor

**Taak 12a: Supervisor Feedback**

-  Open Task 12a in eJournal.
    
-  Find the mandatory standard feedback template.
    
-  Request your final assessment from your workplace supervisor.
    
-  Arrange a feedback discussion.
    
-  Discuss your strengths and areas for improvement.
    
-  Make sure your supervisor completes and publishes the assessment in eJournal.
    
-  Check that the assessment is included in your portfolio.
    

**Important:** You must use the standard feedback template in eJournal.

If you experience technical problems, contact **[gst@uu.nl](mailto:gst@uu.nl)**.

## B. Self-Assessment

**Taak 12b: Zelfreflectie**

-  Open the assessment rubric in eJournal.
    
-  Assess your teaching abilities.
    
-  Reflect on your performance in the relevant tasks.
    
-  Identify your strengths.
    
-  Identify your areas for improvement.
    
-  Explain the reasoning behind your self-assessment.
    
-  Complete the self-reflection in eJournal.
    
-  Submit the task.

## C. Reflection Report

**Length: 1 to 2 A4 pages**

Your report should contain three sections.

### 1. Development

-  Explain how you have developed as a teacher.
    
-  Describe your progress towards your learning goals.
    
-  Discuss your strengths.
    
-  Identify areas where you still need to improve.
    

### 2. Reflection

-  Explain what you learned from teaching.
    
-  Discuss feedback from your supervisor.
    
-  Describe how your teaching practice has changed.
    
-  Connect educational theory to your experiences.
    
-  Explain how you plan to continue improving.
    

### 3. Future Goals

-  Formulate **2 concrete learning goals**.
    
-  Explain how you plan to achieve them.
    
-  Explain why these goals are important for your development as a teacher.
    

### Submission

-  Add your reflection report to Task 12 as instructed in eJournal.
    
-  Check that your final supervisor assessment is included.
    
-  Check that your self-assessment is complete.
    
-  Submit the required Task 12 components.

# 5. How to Record and Upload Teaching Videos

The uploaded instructions explain two methods.

## Option A: Upload an Existing Recording

1. Go to **video.uu.nl**.
    
2. Log in with your Solis ID and two-factor authentication.
    
3. Select **Mijn Kanaal**.
    
4. Click **Inhoud hier toevoegen**.
    
5. Select **Upload**.
    
6. Choose your video.
    
7. Turn off **Importeren als concept** so that the video can be shared.
    
8. Complete the upload.
    

### Add Video to eJournal

-  Open your uploaded video.
    
-  Click the Share button.
    
-  Find **Deze media insluiten**.
    
-  Copy the embed code.
    
-  Paste it into the appropriate eJournal field.
    
-  Check that your supervisor can access the video.
    

---

## Option B: Record Directly Through the UU Video Platform

You can use a smartphone or tablet.

### Checklist

-  Open video.uu.nl in your browser.
    
-  Log in with your Solis ID.
    
-  Click the blue plus button.
    
-  Select Upload.
    
-  Select **Importeren vanaf je apparaat**.
    
-  Choose **Maak foto of neem video op**.
    
-  Select Video.
    
-  Record your lesson.
    
-  Select **Gebruik video**.
    
-  Click **Bestanden uploaden**.
    
-  Open your uploaded recording.
    
-  Adjust publishing settings so that authorized viewers can access it.
    
-  Share the video through eJournal.
    

The instructions state that this recording method uploads the video directly to your personal channel rather than saving it locally on your phone or tablet.

### Privacy Requirements

-  Obtain permission from your supervisor before recording.
    
-  Follow the school's recording and privacy rules.
    
-  Avoid identifiable students unless permission requirements are satisfied.
    
-  Do not share recordings publicly.
    
-  Use the secured university platform.

## 8. Recommended Order of Work

To make completing the portfolio easier, I recommend this sequence:

**First:**

-  Prepare your two lesson plans.
    
-  Arrange recording permission.
    
-  Schedule your supervisor's lesson observation.
    

**Second:**

-  Teach and record your lessons.
    
-  Write your lesson reflections.
    
-  Collect the observation report.
    
-  Upload your video evidence.
    

**Third:**

-  Complete Tasks 1 to 5 and 7.
    
-  Submit each task individually.
    

**Fourth:**

-  Request the final supervisor assessment through eJournal.
    
-  Complete your self-assessment.
    
-  Write your reflection report.
    
-  Complete Task 12.
    

**Finally:**

-  Check the complete portfolio.
    
-  Submit the entire portfolio for assessment.
    

### Most Important Next Step

**Arrange your lesson recordings and supervisor observation first.** These depend on other people's availability and are harder to complete at the last minute. You can then use your lesson plans and recordings for several PiP1a tasks without having to create entirely separate evidence for every task.
