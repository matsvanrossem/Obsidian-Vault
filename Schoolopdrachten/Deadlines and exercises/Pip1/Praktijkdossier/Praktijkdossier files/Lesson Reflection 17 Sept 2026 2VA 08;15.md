

For this reflection, I chose one of the first lessons I taught fully independently. I deliberately chose a grammar lesson on the Past Simple because I expected it to be structured, with clear objectives that would be relatively easy to work towards.

Looking back, not everything in my lesson plan went as intended, although I was able to deliver most of my instruction without major issues. The second half of the lesson, particularly the independent working phase, was more challenging. I struggled with how to respond when most students were working while a few continued to interrupt. I noticed that I did not yet have enough authority in the classroom to consistently encourage these students to cooperate.

Another challenge was managing students who entered inappropriate names in LessonUp. Although I was reasonably satisfied with how I addressed the class, I realised that I need to be more assertive when dealing with disruptive behaviour. I also encountered some limitations with LessonUp, which eventually led me to switch to Obsidian in later lessons.

One positive aspect was the difference I noticed between this 2 VWO class and my 2 HAVO class. The VWO students were more engaged with the material and more vocal about what they wanted to learn. I found this very enjoyable, as it made the lesson feel more interactive.

Overall, I was reasonably satisfied with how the lesson went, especially considering my limited experience teaching independently. My main point for improvement is classroom management, particularly maintaining authority during independent work while still allowing students to engage with the material.
