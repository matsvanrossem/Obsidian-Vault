This video shows the first ten minutes of my Chapter 1 test recap lesson with a 2 VWO class.

I begin by introducing the lesson and explaining the activities using the interactive whiteboard. I then switch to LessonUp, where students join a short diagnostic quiz designed to check their existing knowledge of the material covered in Chapter 1.

The purpose of this activity is to determine which students would benefit from additional instruction and which students are ready for a more challenging assignment. This allows me to differentiate between students based on their individual learning needs.

During the recording, I use the interactive whiteboard to guide the lesson, explain instructions and discuss the activity with the class. Students participate using their laptops, while I remain at the front to monitor the activity and provide guidance.

Looking back at the recording, I am reasonably satisfied with my classroom presence. I appear more comfortable addressing the class than during my earlier lessons, and I make use of gestures and visual support to communicate instructions. However, I can also see that I need to continue working on maintaining students' attention, particularly when they are using their laptops.
