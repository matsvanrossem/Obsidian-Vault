
For this lesson, I wanted to give students the opportunity to prepare for their Chapter 1 test in a way that suited their individual needs. I started with a short LessonUp quiz to identify which students needed extra instruction and which were ready for a more challenging assignment.

I was very satisfied with how this lesson went. Students who needed additional support could receive targeted instruction, while the others worked in small groups to create their own Chapter 1 test. Both groups responded very positively to this approach. I noticed that students appreciated being able to work at their own level, and the creative assignment gave the more confident students something meaningful to do.

I also enjoyed teaching this lesson because the differentiation allowed me to focus more directly on the students who needed my help. Using Obsidian to organise and present the lesson worked well, as it gave students a clear overview of the activities and expectations.

Compared to my earlier lessons, I felt more confident in my ability to organise the classroom and guide students through different activities. This lesson showed me how effective differentiation can be when students are given clear instructions and tasks that match their needs.

Overall, I consider this one of my more successful lessons so far. It reinforced the importance of adapting my teaching to different students rather than expecting everyone to follow exactly the same instruction.
