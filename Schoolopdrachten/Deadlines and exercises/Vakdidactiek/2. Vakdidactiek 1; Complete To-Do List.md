## Assignment 1: Vision of Your Subject

**Handelingsopdracht 1: Vakautobiografie**

**Deadline: 4 September 2026**

**Length: 1,000 to 1,250 words**

### Checklist

-  Read Brandl (2008), _Communicative Language Teaching in Action_.
    
-  Describe your experiences learning foreign languages.
    
-  Reflect on which teaching methods worked best for you.
    
-  Connect your experiences to four principles from Brandl.
    
-  Describe a language teacher you consider a role model.
    
-  Identify the qualities of an effective language teacher.
    
-  Reflect on your existing knowledge and teaching skills.
    
-  Identify areas where you need to develop.
    
-  Formulate two questions about language teaching you want to investigate.
    
-  Write your personal vision of your school subject.
    
-  Submit through Brightspace.
    

**Goal:** Explain what good language teaching means to you and why your subject matters.

## Assignment 2: Textbook Analysis

**Handelingsopdracht 2: Analyse van een leergang**

**Deadline: 11 September 2026**

**Length:**

- Part A: 500 to 600 words.
    
- Part B: 200 to 300 words.
    

### Part A: Textbook Analysis

-  Select a textbook used at your placement school.
    
-  Choose two chapters or two digital teaching units.
    
-  Determine whether the material is skills-based or task-based.
    
-  Select two or three evaluation criteria from the required literature.
    
-  Analyse the textbook using these criteria.
    
-  Evaluate how well the materials support communicative language teaching.
    
-  Reflect on strengths and weaknesses.
    
-  Attach examples of the analysed teaching materials.
    

### Part B: Curriculum Analysis

-  Obtain your school's subject curriculum or departmental teaching plans.
    
-  Examine how the curriculum is structured.
    
-  Identify connections to national curriculum goals.
    
-  Examine how it connects to the CEFR (ERK).
    
-  Evaluate whether the curriculum promotes communicative language teaching.
    
-  Submit both parts through Brightspace.
    

**Useful:** This analysis can provide a starting point for your final Vakdidactiek assignment.

## Assignment 3: Lesson Plan

**Handelingsopdracht 3: Lesplan in de doeltaal**

**Deadline: 25 September 2026**

**Length: 1 to 3 A4 pages**

### Checklist

-  Select a class and lesson to teach.
    
-  Design a lesson using the Model Didactische Analyse (MDA).
    
-  Write the lesson plan in your target language.
    
-  Specify the class level and students' prior knowledge.
    
-  Identify the language skills being practised.
    
-  Write learning objectives using appropriate can-do statements.
    
-  Include student activities.
    
-  Include teacher activities.
    
-  Choose teaching materials and resources.
    
-  Create a realistic time schedule.
    
-  Explain how learning will be assessed.
    
-  Attach all necessary teaching materials.
    
-  Teach the lesson.
    
-  Submit the lesson plan through Brightspace.
    

**Useful:** You can reuse this lesson plan in your PiP1a practice portfolio.

## Assignment 4: Target Language in Teaching

**Handelingsopdracht 4: Doeltaal = leertaal**

### Part A: Teaching Videos

**Deadline: 14 October 2026**

### Checklist

-  Record yourself teaching in two or three different lessons.
    
-  Select two clips from at least two different lessons.
    
-  Make sure each clip lasts between **2 and 5 minutes**.
    
-  Choose one clip involving speaking or conversation activities.
    
-  Choose another clip showing a different teaching situation.
    
-  Demonstrate how you use the target language.
    
-  Analyse your language use in both clips.
    
-  Explain what went well and what could improve.
    
-  Write a short description for each clip, maximum 400 words.
    
-  Formulate at least one specific feedback question per clip.
    
-  Upload both videos to Pitch2Peer through Brightspace.
    

### Part B: Peer Feedback

**Deadline: 30 October 2026**

-  Open Pitch2Peer.
    
-  Watch the videos of your assigned classmates.
    
-  Read their analyses and feedback requests.
    
-  Give constructive feedback on their teaching.
    
-  Submit your feedback through Pitch2Peer.
    
-  Review feedback received on your own videos.

## Final Assignment: Three Consecutive Lessons

**Deadline: 6 January 2027**

This is the main graded assignment for Vakdidactiek 1.

### Preparation

-  Consult your workplace supervisor.
    
-  Select a class.
    
-  Arrange to teach three consecutive lessons.
    
-  Plan an appropriate formative or summative assessment.
    
-  Make sure the lessons and assessment take place during the course semester.
    

### Lesson Design

-  Formulate learning objectives.
    
-  Design three connected lessons.
    
-  Use appropriate teaching methods.
    
-  Incorporate relevant educational theory.
    
-  Select teaching materials.
    
-  Explain how your lessons reflect your vision of language teaching.
    

### Implementation and Evaluation

-  Teach all three lessons.
    
-  Carry out the assessment.
    
-  Collect evidence of student learning.
    
-  Analyse the assessment results.
    
-  Evaluate the effectiveness of your lessons.
    
-  Reflect on your teaching decisions.
    
-  Explain what you would improve.
    

### Submission

-  Consult the detailed assignment instructions and assessment rubric on Brightspace.
    
-  Complete the written final assignment.
    
-  Check references and formatting.
    
-  Submit through Brightspace by **6 January 2027**.
    

The manual confirms the three-lesson and assessment requirements but refers students to Brightspace for the full final-assignment instructions.
