### Lesson sequence, 3 HAVO

| Time      | Phase                        | Content / goal of this phase                                                                                      | Didactic method                              | Student actions                                              | Teacher actions                                                                                        | Materials                      | Check / evaluation                                                     |
| --------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0-3 min   | **Lesson start**             | Establish attention and make the lesson objective clear.                                                          | Whole-class start                            | Prepare materials and read the objective.                    | Welcome students, establish attention and display today's lesson objective.                            | Board/slides                   | Ask one student to restate the lesson objective.                       |
| 3-8 min   | **Activate prior knowledge** | Recall what a subject, verb and auxiliary verb are, and notice how word order changes in questions and negatives. | Whole-class questioning / retrieval practice | Identify differences in word order and auxiliary verbs.      | Ask what changes when a statement becomes a question or a negative sentence. Use simple examples.      | Board/slides                   | Check answers orally and correct misconceptions immediately.           |
| 8-13 min  | **Guided start**             | Introduce the lesson focus through ex. 51.                                                                        | Guided practice                              | Complete ex. 51 and discuss answers.                         | Complete ex. 51 (p. 70) with the class and connect answers to the grammar focus.                       | Coursebook/workbook            | Ask students to explain why an answer is correct.                      |
| 13-20 min | **Instruction: concept**     | Understand the rules for forming questions and negations.                                                         | Direct instruction                           | Listen, take notes and transform example sentences.          | Discuss Grammar 3. Model the sentence structures and highlight auxiliary verbs and word order.         | Board/slides, grammar overview | Use short check-for-understanding questions during instruction.        |
| 20-25 min | **Modeling**                 | See how to apply the rule step by step.                                                                           | Teacher modeling / think-aloud               | Follow the examples and explain the rule.                    | Think aloud while changing statements into questions and negations. Ask students to explain each step. | Board/slides                   | Ask students to predict the next step before revealing it.             |
| 25-35 min | **Independent practice**     | Apply the rules independently.                                                                                    | Individual work / pair support               | Complete ex. 51/52/53/54/55 independently or in pairs.       | Monitor work, give feedback and provide extra instruction where needed.                                | Coursebook/workbook            | Check student work while circulating and note common errors.           |
| 35-40 min | **Check for understanding**  | Explain and justify correct grammar choices.                                                                      | Whole-class feedback                         | Give answers and justify their choices.                      | Check selected answers and ask students to explain the word order and auxiliary verb used.             | Coursebook/workbook, board     | Listen for correct explanations and reteach if needed.                 |
| 40-43 min | **Writing check**            | Demonstrate independent control of questions and negations.                                                       | Individual formative assessment              | Write the transformed sentences.                             | Give two short statements and ask students to write one question and one negative sentence from each.  | Board/slides, notebook         | Collect or quickly check responses against the success criteria.       |
| 43-44 min | **Lesson closure**           | Summarise the key grammar rules.                                                                                  | Whole-class recap                            | Summarise one rule for questions and one rule for negations. | Return to the objective and ask students to explain one rule for questions and one for negations.      | Board/slides                   | Check whether students can state the rule without support.             |
| 44-45 min | **Exit ticket / homework**   | Confirm individual understanding and set homework.                                                                | Exit ticket                                  | Complete the exit ticket and write down homework.            | Give one final sentence to transform. Remind students of the homework.                                 | Board/slides                   | Use the exit ticket to judge whether the lesson objective was reached. |
